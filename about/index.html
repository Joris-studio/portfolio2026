About

Joris van den Ende

I’m Joris van den Ende, a Senior Product Designer & Design Lead. I design complex digital products and lead design teams while staying hands-on.

I was Head of Design at FLYR Labs, where I helped grow the design team from one to seven people and designed revenue management software for airlines. In 2026, I worked as Design Lead at Dot Control on projects for CBR and Verpact. I also worked on Mijnpensioenoverzicht through Frog Design.

My work covers interaction design, design systems and helping designers develop in their roles.
