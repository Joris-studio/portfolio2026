Statiegeld

The wallet closes the loop

Deposit returns were fragmented, with rewards often ending as a paper receipt. We designed an app that brings checking, scanning and finding a return point together, and worked out how a digital wallet could connect scanning, saving and rewards into an experience worth returning to.

My part

The product concept and the design of the app: scanning, the result of a scan, the wallet and finding a return point.

Returning a bottle is easy once. Making it a habit is the design problem.

A single return is a loose action: you bring the bottle back, and the value disappears into a paper receipt. Returning again and again asks for an experience that hangs together.

The app connects three things that used to be separate: scanning, saving and rewards.

Every scan adds visible value

The Stash is the centre of the experience. A scan ends with the credited amount and the new Stash balance. From there, users can scan another item or go to the Stash to see what they have built up and cash out.

This flow was worked out as wireframes. The amounts are example values from the design; this is the design intention, not measured behaviour.

From a bottle to the next return point

1 · Scan

Scanning is the entry point

No deposit logo on the bottle or can? Statie Check opens the camera to scan the packaging, with manual entry as an alternative.

2 · Result

A scan ends with a clear result

Each success message names the item and shows its deposit value, then explains where this kind of packaging can be returned. Two actions follow: scan another package, or find a return point.

Success message designs for the app, marked Ready for Dev.

3 · Return

Finding the next return point

StatieMap on the home screen leads to the Kaart tab: a map of return points across the Netherlands, searchable by place or postcode.

Outcome

Live in the app stores

The Statiegeld app is live in the app stores. This case shows screens from the live app (home, Statie Check, the scanner and the map), success messages designed for the app and marked Ready for Dev, and a wallet flow worked out as wireframes.
